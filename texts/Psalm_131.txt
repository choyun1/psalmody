Psalm 131 | Domine, non est
% unpointed draft: the words of the 1979 BCP. Pointing for Anglican chant goes in your parish folder
% (parish/texts/), or use the pointing editor in the chant browser; plainchant tones need none.

1   O LORD, I am not proud; *
      I have no haughty looks.

2   I do not occupy myself with great matters, *
      or with things that are too hard for me.

3   But I still my soul and make it quiet, like a child upon its mother's breast; *
      my soul is quieted within me.

4   O Israel, wait upon the LORD, *
      from this time forth for evermore.
