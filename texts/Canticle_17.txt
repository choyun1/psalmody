Canticle 17 | The Song of Simeon (Luke 2:29-32)
% unpointed draft: the words of the 1979 BCP. Pointing for Anglican chant goes in your parish folder
% (parish/texts/), or use the pointing editor in the chant browser; plainchant tones need none.
% Nunc Dimittis

1   Lord, you now have set your servant free *
      to go in peace as you have promised;

2   For these eyes of mine have seen the Savior, *
      whom you have prepared for all the world to see:

3   A Light to enlighten the nations, *
      and the glory of your people Israel.
