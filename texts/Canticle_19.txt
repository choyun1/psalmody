Canticle 19 | The Song of the Redeemed (Revelation 15:3-4)
% unpointed draft: the words of the 1979 BCP. Pointing for Anglican chant goes in your parish folder
% (parish/texts/), or use the pointing editor in the chant browser; plainchant tones need none.
% Magna et mirabilia

1   O ruler of the universe, Lord God, great deeds are they that you have done, *
      surpassing human understanding.

2   Your ways are ways of righteousness and truth, *
      O King of all the ages.

3   Who can fail to do you homage, Lord, and sing the praises of your Name? *
      for you only are the Holy One.

4   All nations will draw near and fall down before you, *
      because your just and holy works have been revealed.
