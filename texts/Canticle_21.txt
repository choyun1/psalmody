Canticle 21 | You are God
% unpointed draft: the words of the 1979 BCP. Pointing for Anglican chant goes in your parish folder
% (parish/texts/), or use the pointing editor in the chant browser; plainchant tones need none.
% Te Deum laudamus
% the verse division and * are editorial: the BCP prints this canticle as prose
% no Gloria Patri (the BCP prints none with this canticle)

1   You are God: *
      we praise you;

2   You are the Lord; *
      we acclaim you;

3   You are the eternal Father: *
      All creation worships you.

4   To you all angels, all the powers of heaven, *
      Cherubim and Seraphim, sing in endless praise:

5   Holy, holy, holy Lord, God of power and might, *
      heaven and earth are full of your glory.

6   The glorious company of apostles *
      praise you.

7   The noble fellowship of prophets *
      praise you.

8   The white-robed army of martyrs *
      praise you.

9   Throughout the world the holy Church *
      acclaims you;

10  Father, *
      of majesty unbounded,

11  your true and only Son, *
      worthy of all worship,

12  and the Holy Spirit, *
      advocate and guide.

13  You, Christ, are the king of glory, *
      the eternal Son of the Father.

14  When you became man to set us free *
      you did not shun the Virgin’s womb.

15  You overcame the sting of death *
      and opened the kingdom of heaven to all believers.

16  You are seated at God’s right hand *
      in glory.

17  We believe that you will come *
      and be our judge.

18  Come then, Lord, and help your people, *
      bought with the price of your own blood,

19  and bring us with your saints *
      to glory everlasting.
